Biopalm Automation

Biopalm Automation is an internal tool of Biopalm Education (ไบโอปาล์ม เอ็ดดูเคชั่น). It is used only by Biopalm staff, with Biopalm's own Google accounts, to:

  • copy recordings of our own online classes between our Google Drive folders;
  • upload those recordings as private videos to Biopalm's own YouTube channel and add them to a private playlist;
  • read responses from Biopalm's own Google Forms.

It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from anyone outside Biopalm.
